1Cleanroom garment stores — phone-first mockups

Working assumptions, for you to correct:

  • Built for phones and handheld scanners first — one column, touch targets 44px and up. The same layouts widen for tablet and desktop.
  • One stock item = garment × color × size × location (Fab1 / Fab2). Every stocked item has its own minimum.
  • Deliveries come in as bulk counts. Issuing is one scan per garment, always with the badge scanned first.
  • The mockups are live: issuing in 1d takes stock out of 1f–1h, and minimums you change in 1g show up in 1f.
